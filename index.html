Selected design cases

Two mobile app MVPs, a usability audit of DB Navigator and a website for a bakery. The first three start from user research.

Mayak

Mayak

Multilingual emotional support for immigrants in Germany

A survey of 26 immigrants and three interviews showed that adapting is not only about language and paperwork. 42% rated their stress above 7 out of 10, and half had no safe place to talk. The MVP has 308 screens in four languages and a clickable prototype of 420 frames.

  • Project typeConcept project
  • PlatformApp + landing page
  • RoleUX/UI designer
  • ScopeFrom research to MVP
Read the case: Mayak
RoomRoots

RoomRoots

AI-assisted plant selection, from discovery to checkout

Research into how people choose plants showed they want fewer, better-matched options and clear care guidance. RoomRoots narrows the catalogue by light, room conditions, care experience and pets, with personalised recommendations and an AI-Vision feature.

  • Project typeMVP concept
  • PlatformMobile app
  • RoleUX/UI designer
  • ScopeFrom research to MVP
Read the case: RoomRoots
DB Navigator

DB Navigator

An unsolicited redesign of Deutsche Bahn’s journey planner

Short transfers, delays and date filters that are easy to set wrong make journeys hard to plan. Two participants planned trips with transfers and replanned after delays; I compared the flows with FlixBus, Trainline and Ukrzaliznytsia. The redesign is in progress.

  • Project typeUnsolicited redesign
  • PlatformMobile app
  • RoleUX/UI designer
  • ScopeResearch, testing, redesign
Read the case: DB Navigator

Muka

A local bakery with daily baking and same-day delivery. The interface stays restrained and relies on material, texture and traces of work, giving the product space before any call to action appears.

User needs, clear product logic

Three parts of my work, each with the question I start it with.

  1. “What problem are people actually trying to solve?”

    Research and structure

    I turn user needs and research findings into clear priorities, product logic and content structure.

    User research · Content structure · JTBD, CJM

  2. “Can every next step feel clear and effortless?”

    Interaction and usability

    I design user flows, responsive interfaces and thoughtful states that take friction out of the journey.

    User flows · Usability testing · Prototyping

  3. “Does every screen speak the same visual language?”

    Visual systems

    I build consistent visual systems and reusable components that support usability and product growth.

    Design systems · UI components · Brand
