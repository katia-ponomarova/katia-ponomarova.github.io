Hosting and server log files

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (GitHub privacy statement). When you open a page, your browser sends technical data that the host stores in server log files: your IP address, the date and time, the page you requested, the page you came from, and your browser and operating system.

These data are needed to deliver the site and keep it secure. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: my legitimate interest in a website that works and is protected against attacks. The host deletes the log files after a period that it sets itself. I do not combine them with other data.

The host is based in the USA and certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, so data may be transferred there on the basis of the European Commission’s adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser (no local storage or session storage). It uses no analytics, no advertising and no social media plugins. The language versions are separate pages, so your choice of language is not stored either.

Fonts and scripts

The fonts (Oswald, Poppins, Caveat and Montserrat) and the animation scripts (GSAP and Lenis) are stored on this site’s own server. Your browser does not connect to Google Fonts or any other content delivery network.

Contact by email or phone

If you write to me or call me, I use your details (your name, email address or phone number, and what you tell me) only to answer you and, if you contact me about a job, for the application process. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR for steps before a possible contract, and otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in answering you. I delete these data when they are no longer needed, unless the law requires me to keep them.

My email account is provided by Google (Gmail), so emails to me are processed on Google’s servers.

Links to other websites

The links to LinkedIn, Behance and Telegram are plain links. No data are sent to these services until you click one. After that, their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have them corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17), to restrict their processing (Art. 18) and to receive them in a portable format (Art. 20). Where I process data on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, you can object at any time (Art. 21). To use these rights, write to me at the email address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for me is the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz, Hintere Bleiche 34, 55116 Mainz.

I do not use automated decision-making or profiling.
