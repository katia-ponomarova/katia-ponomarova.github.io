RoomRoots

AI plant matching by light, room type, care level and pets

An MVP e-commerce mobile app that helps people choose and buy plants based on their space, lighting, care experience and preferences. Personalised recommendations and AI-Vision reduce uncertainty and guide users from discovery to checkout.

01 · The problem

Plenty of plants, no guidance

The plant market is growing and there is plenty to choose from, but most shops show everyone the same catalogue and ignore the buyer’s conditions. This hits beginners hardest: they make mistakes and lose interest in buying.

Three questions a buyer can’t answer

  • Will I manage to care for it?

  • Will it suit my space?

  • Is there enough light in the room?

Four barriers to buying

  1. 01

    Lack of personalisation

    Light, room type and experience level are not taken into account. Everyone sees the same catalogue.

  2. 02

    Too many options

    A wide choice makes the decision harder: people get lost among the options.

  3. 03

    No contextual guidance

    Nothing explains which plant suits this particular space. The decision is left to the buyer.

  4. 04

    Fear of making a mistake

    Plants that died before make people afraid to buy again, so they avoid choosing at all.

02 · Research

What the research revealed

The main problem is not a lack of choice, but a lack of confidence. Users need fewer, more relevant options and clear guidance connected to their space, care experience and everyday conditions.

Target audience

Age and home
25–40living in flats or houses
Three kinds of buyers
  • Beginners with no experience
  • People with basic knowledge
  • People who design interiors
Shared motivation
Whatever their experience, they want a plant that fits the interior, suits the room’s conditions and needs no complicated care.

They want to be sure the plant will survive and look good.

Research foundation

  1. 1

    A growing plant market, and catalogues that ignore the buyer’s conditions.

  2. 2

    Target audience

  3. 3

    Anna, 29, an office manager in Köln who has lost plants before.

04 · MVP scope

The MVP in six functions

For the MVP I kept only the key functions needed to test the purchase hypothesis.

  1. 01

    Catalog

    Browse the available plants with basic information and a quick choice.

  2. 02

    Product card

    Everything about the plant: conditions, care and recommendations.

  3. 03

    Cart

    Check the chosen plants before placing the order.

  4. 04

    AI-Vision

    Plants matched to the user’s conditions or to a photo of their space.

  5. 05

    Checkout

    Enter the details and complete the purchase.

  6. 06

    Confirmation

    A notice that the order went through, with its details.

07 · Outcome

Project conclusions

The MVP focuses on making the choice simpler and less uncertain.

The project in short

  1. 01 · ProblemConfidence

    Buyers need fewer, better-matched plants and clear care guidance.

  2. 02 · SolutionRoomRoots

    A short quiz and AI-Vision narrow the catalog by light, room conditions, care experience and pets.

  3. 03 · MVP6

    core functions on one UI kit, from the catalog to the order confirmation, in a hi-fi prototype.

Four conclusions

The whole path, from the first tap to a paid order, is mapped and prototyped, and one UI kit keeps the interface consistent so it can grow with the product.

RoomRoots turns plant shopping into a simple, personalised and confident experience.

  1. 01

    Simplicity builds confidence

  2. 02

    Personalisation makes the choice relevant

  3. 03

    Context supports better decisions

  4. 04

    A clear flow strengthens trust
